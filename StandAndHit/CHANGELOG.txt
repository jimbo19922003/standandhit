Version 1.0.0
October 19, 2026

Here's what's changed since the first release changelog.

Stand and Hit (S&H) has its own StandAndHit folder and saved settings. Use
/snh or /standandhit to open it. The rotation tabs are Hit Button, Hit Button 2,
Hit Button 3, and Hit Button 4. If you're moving from an earlier addon, export
your rotations before removing it, then import them tab by tab and click
Prepare. You'll also need to recreate your action-bar macros, assign your
direct keys, and choose your local preferences after installing.

Extra rotations

I added extra Hit Buttons after realizing I wanted separate rotations for
grinding, dungeon trash, dungeon bosses, and other situations. Hit Button,
Hit Button 2, Hit Button 3, and Hit Button 4 each have their own setup tab,
rules, macro, cast key, and saved button position. The three extra rotations
can be enabled or disabled separately.
The extra rotations start disabled and empty, so you can build each one from
scratch or import an older export.

Conditions and casting

- Shoot now recognizes when you're using a wand. You can keep pressing without
  toggling it off, and it'll try again if the wand stops firing. A matching
  rule higher in the list still takes priority.
- Added AND, OR, and NOT conditions. You can require several buffs or debuffs,
  give a spell another way to trigger, or stop it from being chosen when a
  condition matches. An OR match still has to pass your AND and NOT checks.
- You can check whether your tracked debuffs are present or missing on your
  current living hostile target, as well as checking tracked auras.
- Unreadable live debuff details no longer cause a false CHECK SETUP warning
  after preparation. The addon still checks that it's using the right tracker
  and settings. Other setup errors now include a short explanation.
- Added + Casting options > Combo points for any spell, including builders.
  Choose >=, <=, =, or != and a number from 0 to 5. For example, a builder can
  use IF Always with combo points != 5, while a finisher uses >= 5.
  This adds an AND requirement, so an OR match can't skip it.
  It checks your current target regardless of Cast on and needs no Cooldown
  Manager tracker or individual spell slot on an action bar.
- Choose Highest learned or a specific learned rank with the spell rank selector.
- Off cooldown works for spells that have a cooldown but don't apply an aura.
- Set a completed-cast limit from 1 to 99 for supported cast-time spells.
  Failed or interrupted casts don't use up a count.
- Conditions have numbers, and Attach count to lets you choose which eligible
  tracked-aura IF, OR, or AND condition starts a cast batch. An OR proc without
  a count can still allow the spell without starting a batch of its own. If a
  batch is already running, matching completed casts during that proc count
  toward it too. Combo points is separate from Cast Count; you can't attach a
  cast count to a combo-point condition.
- Stop current cast lets a present-proc condition interrupt another cast.
  Stop if buffs change interrupts when the last requested rule's aura
  conditions no longer match. Both need a fresh press of your direct cast key
  or assigned controller button, and keep channels protected.
- Set cast key and Clear key give each rotation its own direct binding. The
  addon checks for duplicate keys between active rotations.

Interface

- The Controller window lets you assign a gamepad button to each Hit Button.
  These use the direct casting controls, including interrupt options. You can
  keep a keyboard cast key assigned to the same rotation too.
- You can enable rumble when combat starts or Rumble on crit for your outgoing
  critical damage. There's a rumble test in the window. Crit alerts depend on
  the game exposing readable events; the window tells you if they're unavailable.
- Shortened Hit Button tooltips to the last selected spell and directions.
  Ctrl+left-click hides or shows the tooltip and remembers your choice for
  each button. It works in combat and doesn't cast a spell.
- Enable tracker beside Prepare switches Blizzard's Cooldown Manager on or
  off. It's red when the tracker is disabled, even if you changed that in the
  game's settings.
- The editor has a spell priority list on the left. Select a spell to see its
  settings, so you don't have every rule's controls taking up the screen at once.
- New rules start with IF. The + Casting options menu adds AND, OR, NOT,
  Combo points, Cast Count, or other casting settings as needed. Adding Combo
  points to a blank rule selects Always automatically. Its comparison, number,
  and Remove button are right there in the editor.
- Added conditions appear in the editor with their own numbers and controls.
  Long rules scroll, and adding a condition brings it into view.
- Rotation textual explanation describes the selected tab's draft from top
  to bottom. Each condition gets a bullet, with IF, AND, OR, and NOT emphasized
  to make the logic easier to follow.
- Removed the detailed cast-batch and stop-casting notes from that explanation.
  Cast limits keep the short "AND fewer than ... successful
  casts ... have counted for this batch" line under the attached condition.
- Copy to clipboard lets you copy the explanation. If the client can't copy
  directly, it opens selected text so you can press Ctrl+C.
- Import and Export let you share a rotation or keep a copy of your draft.
  They include rule order, conditions, ranks, cast counts, casting options,
  and Protect channels. Importing replaces the selected tab's draft. You'll
  still need to set up your trackers and click Prepare on that character.
- Exports include combo-point comparisons and numbers, and the textual
  explanation shows them too. New combo exports use SNH2; rotations without
  combo conditions use SNH1. You can still import older rotation exports.
- When preparation is needed, a notice near the top of the screen tells you
  why and offers Prepare or Dismiss. It handles the affected rotations
  together. Any unfinished draft edits stay separate from the saved rotations
  it reapplies.
- Added an optional Hide casting errors checkbox for each Hit Button. It
  briefly hides common red casting errors, their character voice lines, and
  error sounds after a spell attempt. You don't need to Prepare for this
  setting to work. Other messages and audio stay as they were, and the
  preference stays local when you share a rotation.
- Simplified the minimap artwork and lowered its resolution so it fits better
  beside the game's other minimap buttons.

Fixes

- Removed unnecessary crit-rumble setup calls when entering combat, including
  when the option is off. Event setup now waits until you're out of combat.
- Canceling keyboard assignment in combat now releases the keyboard without
  trying to change protected input settings.
- Fixed a false CHECK SETUP warning when the game hides a target tracker's
  live linked aura ID during combat. The addon still checks readable ID changes
  and the required tracker settings.
- Reduced memory growth while idle by reusing tracker-check data and avoiding
  repeated full spell-information tables. It still checks every half-second
  for changed or unavailable trackers.
- Restore at login now waits for talent and spell data to finish loading and
  settle before restoring your prepared rotations. This clears false change
  warnings from loading. Actual talent changes, learned spells, and required
  setup changes still ask you to Prepare.
- Generated macros and direct cast keys for rotations using Combo points
  clear the spell queue on every press before choosing again. Queue clearing
  doesn't stop a cast that's already underway.
- Temporary tracker changes while changing zones can now recover outside
  combat without making you click Prepare again. Talent changes, learned
  spells, and required setup changes still prompt for preparation.
- Fixed cooldown-only rules being skipped during the global cooldown. This
  could leave you spamming a filler spell even though a higher-priority spell
  like Arcane Wrath was ready. The cooldown check now tells the global cooldown
  apart from the spell's own cooldown across all four rotations.
- Fixed valid procs being rejected because the tracked talent or spell has a
  different name from the buff it gives you.
- Fixed interrupt options being unavailable for a present-buff OR condition.
  Interrupts now use the direct casting controls.
- Fixed the textual explanation opening behind the main settings window.
- Removed the need to open a separate editor to see added conditions.

Current limits and planned work

Aura and cooldown conditions still need supported entries in Blizzard's
trackers. Combo-point conditions don't need those trackers or individual
spell slots.

Completed-cast limits currently work with normal cast-time spells. The exact
spell rank has to stay in a fixed slot on a visible Blizzard action bar.
You can't attach counts to target debuffs, NOT exclusions, Always, cooldown,
or combo-point conditions.

Rogue tests confirmed the combo-point signal in combat. Selecting and
casting spells with it in the main addon still needs in-game confirmation.

Holding a key keeps the spell it first selected. I'm still looking into
changing spells while the key stays held. For now, release and press again.
I also plan to add item-based cooldowns once I have a suitable item to test.
Most in-game testing so far has been on a mage, including Missile Barrage
and Eureka!, so I'd like to test more classes and spells too.
