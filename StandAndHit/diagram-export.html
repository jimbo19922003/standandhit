Stand and Hit: export your rotation diagram

Copy the diagram from WoW, paste it here, and save the whole rotation as one image.

1. Copy and paste

In Stand and Hit, open Rotation explanation, choose Function Block Diagram, then click Export image. Press Ctrl+C.

This page works offline. Your rotation stays in this browser.

Paste the export to begin.

2. Save the image

PNG is ready to post in Discord. SVG keeps the original size and can be opened in a browser.

Your entire diagram will appear here.
Scroll through the preview to check it before saving.

WoW's screenshot button saves what is on screen. This exporter includes every block, even the parts you had to scroll to see.
